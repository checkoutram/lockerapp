# Privacy Policy - vLocker

**Effective Date:** 8 August 2026

> **Key Summary:** vLocker is a **100% offline** bank locker inventory app. All your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server, cloud, or third party.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

vLocker does **not** collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics. The app operates entirely on your device with:

- **PIN code:** Stored locally (hashed with SHA-256) for app security only
- **Security questions & answers:** Stored locally (answers are hashed) for PIN recovery only
- **Locker inventory data:** Item names, descriptions, photos, and locker status stored only in local device storage
- **Biometric preferences:** Stored locally to enable/disable biometric login

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Since all data is stored locally on your device, vLocker uses your data only to:

- Display your bank locker inventory
- Authenticate you with PIN or biometric (fingerprint/face) lock
- Allow PIN recovery via security questions

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

**All data is stored locally on your device.** vLocker does not:

- Use any cloud services or remote servers
- Send data over the internet
- Share data with third parties
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Include advertisements

Your photos, item details, and PIN are stored in the app's secure local storage on your device only.

---

## 4. Camera & Photos Permission

vLocker requests access to your camera to take photos of items and bills for your locker inventory. These photos are:

- Stored only in the app's local storage
- Never uploaded to any server or cloud
- Never shared with third parties

---

## 5. Biometric Authentication

vLocker supports device biometric authentication (fingerprint/face). This feature:

- Uses your device's native biometric hardware only
- Does not collect or store biometric data
- Can be enabled/disabled in app settings

---

## 6. Data Backup & Export

vLocker provides an optional export feature that allows you to save your data as a JSON file to your device. This export:

- Is initiated only by you
- Is stored in your device's Downloads folder
- Is your responsibility to secure

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

vLocker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

---

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
